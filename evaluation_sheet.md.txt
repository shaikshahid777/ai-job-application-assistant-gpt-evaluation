# GPT Evaluation Sheet — Topic 9

## 1. Evaluation Overview

This evaluation measures the AI Job Application Assistant across three dimensions:

- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Consistency

Each response is scored on a 1–5 scale using the objective rubrics below.

---

## 2. Scoring Rubrics

### Accuracy

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Fully correct, evidence-based, and follows the Knowledge Guide and user-provided information. No factual or unsupported claims. |
| 4 | Correct overall with only a minor omission or small imprecision that does not affect the answer. |
| 3 | Mostly correct but contains a noticeable omission, ambiguity, or limited weakness. |
| 2 | Significant inaccuracies, unsupported assumptions, or important missing information affect the usefulness of the answer. |
| 1 | Fundamentally incorrect, fabricated, or contradicts the Knowledge Guide, user information, or stated requirements. |

### Clarity

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Clear, concise, well-structured, professional, and immediately understandable. |
| 4 | Clear and professional with minor wording, structure, or verbosity issues. |
| 3 | Understandable but somewhat vague, repetitive, or less organized than ideal. |
| 2 | Difficult to follow because of significant ambiguity, unnecessary complexity, or poor organization. |
| 1 | Confusing, contradictory, or extremely difficult to understand. |

### Consistency

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Fully consistent with the GPT's defined rules, previous behavior, terminology, and safety requirements. |
| 4 | Consistent overall with only a minor variation that does not change the meaning or behavior. |
| 3 | Some noticeable inconsistency in wording, reasoning, or application of rules. |
| 2 | Significant inconsistency with established rules or previous behavior. |
| 1 | Contradicts established instructions, rules, or previously demonstrated behavior. |

## 4. Scoring Method

Each response receives one score from 1–5 for Accuracy, Clarity, and Consistency.

The scores will be based only on the defined criteria and the GPT's actual response.

No score will be lowered or increased simply to create an artificial weak area.

After all responses are scored, the average score for each metric will be calculated.

---

## 5. Weak Areas Identified

No significant weak-performing metric was identified in the 10-response evaluation.

- Clarity: 5.0 / 5
- Consistency: 5.0 / 5

Because no functional weakness was identified, no artificial weakness was introduced into the evaluation. Instead, optimization will focus on maintaining the current performance while improving evaluation transparency and response-quality consistency.
